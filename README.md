# Awesome-Compensation-Management-Platform

## Top Compensation Management Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Salary Benchmarking, Pay Equity, Merit Cycles, Equity Compensation, Total Rewards & Compensation Planning*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Compensation Management**. These tools help HR and total rewards teams benchmark salaries, build pay ranges, run merit and bonus cycles, manage equity, analyze pay equity, and communicate total compensation in a data-driven, compliant way.



**Examples** include Pave, Ravio, Figures, Option Impact, Radford (McLagan), PayScale, Salary.com, Compa, OpenComp, CompXL, Payfactors, beqom, Workday Compensation, Compport, HRSoft, Oracle Compensation, and related CompAnalyst / Peakon / Visier offerings (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for salary transparency, payroll/HRIS foundations, offer comparison, and compensation analytics — ideal for organizations seeking transparency, internal pay equity tools, or customizable building blocks without proprietary lock-in.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Frappe HR / ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/hrms)**  

  Fully open-source HR and Payroll system with salary structures, payroll processing, taxation, employee lifecycle, and foundational compensation management capabilities that can be extended for custom needs.



- **[CeleryBand](https://github.com/maybeanerd/celeryband)**  

  Open-source platform for internal salary transparency that allows employees to anonymously share and compare compensation within an organization, with band visualizations and privacy-focused design.



- **[Comparator](https://github.com/DevonPeroutky/comparator)**  

  Free and open-source compensation comparison tool focused on evaluating job offers (especially tech/startup equity), with scenario modeling for dilution and funding rounds that runs entirely in the browser.



- **[Open Salaries resources](https://github.com/sizovs/open-salaries)**  

  Curated collection of companies, case studies, tools, and resources for implementing transparent and open salary policies.



- **[Compensation Benchmarker agents & notebooks](https://github.com/search?q=compensation+benchmarking)**  

  Community AI agents, Jupyter notebooks, and ML frameworks for salary benchmarking, pay equity analysis, anomaly detection, and market pricing using public or proprietary datasets.



- **[Comp Pulse & directional trackers](https://github.com/jschulman/comp-pulse)**  

  Open-source dashboards that track compensation trends and drift (directional signals rather than absolute salaries) using public job-board data.



- **[Flexile / equity & contractor tools](https://github.com/search?q=open+source+equity+compensation)**  

  Emerging open-source projects for managing contractor payments, equity grants, and total compensation components in distributed teams.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **HRIS & payroll foundations**: Odoo Community HR/Payroll modules, other ERPNext customizations, and self-hosted payroll engines.

- **Pay equity & analytics**: Python/R scripts and Streamlit apps for com pa-ratio analysis, regression-based equity studies, and band modeling.

- **Offer & total rewards calculators**: Browser-based tools for modeling base + bonus + equity scenarios.

- **Transparency initiatives**: Internal salary-sharing platforms and policy templates inspired by open-salary companies.

- **Data pipelines**: Tools for ingesting public compensation surveys, job postings, and cost-of-living data for custom benchmarking.

- Many research repositories focused on **fair pay algorithms**, **gender/ethnicity pay gap analysis**, and **explainable compensation models**.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with **Frappe HR / ERPNext** for core employee and salary structure management, layer **CeleryBand-style transparency** or custom dashboards for internal equity, use **Comparator-like tools** for candidate offer modeling, and add analytics notebooks or agents for market benchmarking and pay equity studies. Integrate with existing HRIS via APIs for a complete internal compensation stack.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Compensation systems handle highly sensitive personal and pay data and must comply with data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), pay transparency regulations, and equal pay requirements.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require strong access controls, anonymization practices, audit logging, and legal review before use in formal compensation decision-making or employee-facing transparency programs.
